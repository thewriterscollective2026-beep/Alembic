How it works

Three steps. Zero dashboards.

1

Connect your mailbox

Sign in with Google, or connect any IMAP inbox (Hostinger, Zoho, Outlook). Pick which newsletters you want Alembic to read from — nothing else is touched.

2

Review by email

On your schedule, Alembic researches your sources and emails you a summary, then a full draft. Reply "approved" to move it forward, or reply with feedback and it revises — all from your own inbox.

3

It sends itself

Once you approve, your newsletter goes out to your recipient list — branded, formatted, with unsubscribe handling and delivery tracking built in. No further action needed.

FAQ

Good to know

Can it send anything without me approving it?

No. Every stage — the research summary and the drafted newsletter — is emailed to you for a reply first. There is no automatic send path that skips your approval.

What access does it need to my mailbox?

Read access, to find and read your selected newsletters, and send access, to email you drafts and watch for your reply — within your own inbox only. You can revoke access at any time from your Google Account settings.

Is my data used to train AI models?

No. Your data is used only to generate your newsletter draft — never to train AI models, ours or any third party's, and never sold or shared for advertising. See our Privacy Policy for details.
