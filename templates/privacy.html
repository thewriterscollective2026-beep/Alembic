Alembic ("the App") is operated by Tuesdayscorp ("we", "us"). This policy explains what data the App accesses, why, and how it is used when you connect your Google (Gmail) account.

What we access, and why

When you connect your Gmail account via Google Sign-In, the App requests permission to:

  • Read your mailbox (gmail.readonly) — to find newsletters you're subscribed to and read new messages from the senders you select, so the App can summarize and repurpose them into a new newsletter on your behalf.
  • Send mail as you (gmail.send) — to send you the research summary and draft newsletter for your approval, and to watch for your reply (e.g. "approved"), all within your own mailbox. The App never sends anything without your explicit reply-based approval.

How your data is used

Email content you've selected is sent to Anthropic's Claude API solely to generate your newsletter summary and draft, and may trigger a live web search (via Claude's web search tool) to research related context for that draft. We do not use Google user data for any purpose beyond providing this specific feature.

We do not use your Google user data to train AI models, our own or anyone else's. Anthropic's commercial API terms likewise state that API data is not used to train their models by default. We do not sell Google user data, and we do not share it with any third party for advertising or any purpose other than generating your newsletter draft as described here.

Google user data obtained through Gmail scopes is used only to provide and improve the App's own newsletter-drafting features described above, consistent with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Third parties we use

  • Anthropic (Claude API) — summarizes source content and drafts newsletters.
  • Resend — delivers the final newsletter to your recipient list (not your Gmail account's own sending).
  • Neon — hosts the application's database (your account settings, drafts, recipient list).
  • Google — authentication and, if you connect Gmail, mailbox access as described above.

We do not sell your data or your recipients' data to any third party.

Data retention

Drafts, settings, and your recipient list are retained in our database for as long as your account is active. You can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time by contacting us (below). You can revoke the App's access to your Google account at any time from your Google Account permissions page.

Recipient data

If you upload a recipient list to send newsletters to, those recipients' email addresses are stored to manage sending and honor unsubscribe requests. Every newsletter includes an unsubscribe link; recipients who unsubscribe are permanently excluded from future sends.
